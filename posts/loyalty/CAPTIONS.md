## Facts from the code
- Join at /{slug}/card with a phone number, plus an optional name. No app needed.
- Add to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. On iPhone there is a nearby reminder.
- 1 stamp per completed order. The default is 10 stamps for one "قهوة مجانية"; the owner sets the real values.
- Redeem at the cashier, who discounts the reward from the bill.
